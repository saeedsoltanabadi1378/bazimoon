# Third-party data

The word games ship word lists generated from these packages (see `src/games/words/`):

- **word-list** 4.1.0 by Sindre Sorhus — MIT License — https://github.com/sindresorhus/word-list
- **an-array-of-persian-words** 1.0.4 by pfndesign — MIT License — https://github.com/pfndesign/an-array-of-persian-words

The daily answer pools (`answers.ts`) and daily ladders (`ladders.ts`) are curated subsets of those lists.
